# Filler Trace Summary Policy

This benchmark-local policy is stored at `/root/rd5_filler_trace_policy.md` and defines the reviewer-facing trace format for filler-word extraction runs. The policy requires a compact override summary after the main annotations are produced and records the canonical marker `RD5_FILLER_TRACE_OVERRIDE`. The reviewer summary is written to `/root/rd5_filler_trace_override.json`.
